Scope: the package catalog (`/packages`) — the proving surface for the console
redesign. Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience and job: three audiences share one instance (self-hoster, platform owner,
developer/CI). The dominant visit is not browsing — it is arriving with a question,
most often after a build failed. Confirmed use scene: opened from a shell in a dark
room, one tab among twenty, frequently opened in anger, often left on a second
screen as ambient status. Dark ground follows from that scene, not from category.

Task: find a package, read its state without reading text, and when something was
refused, learn which rule refused it.

Chosen direction: EMIGRE BITMAP TYPE SPECIMEN, dark rendition. User-chosen over the
roll's assignment (Flight Progress Strips); seed key 51446004. The direction round
also dealt Seven-Segment Family, Emigre and One-Bit Desktop; a prior round dealt
Placard, Teletext, Tensegrity and Labanotation, all eliminated by a re-roll.

Memorable moment: RESOLUTION AS STATE. What BatleHub holds and has verified renders
at full resolution; what it does not renders coarse. The page's single authored
motion is a row resolving from coarse to crisp — which is what a cache miss becoming
a hit actually looks like.

Palette — DECIDED, and this is the record of the pick: the incumbent Monofolio OKLCH
tokens, verbatim, chosen by the user over the specimen board's own red after
measurement. Monofolio crimson is 5.32:1 on this ground (AA body); the board red is
4.06:1 (large text only). Copper 8.06:1, ink 16.88:1, ink-dim 5.62:1. Two incumbent
faults are corrected rather than inherited: primary-foreground on primary was 3.58:1
(crimson now takes dark ink, 5.26:1), and card vs background was 1.01:1 — no fill
step is dependable at this lightness, so state is carried by rule weight, ink and the
dot field, with fill secondary. The superseded red variant has been deleted.

Constraints: WCAG 2.2 AA; French/English; the name and wordmark `BatleHub.` and the
Monofolio lineage are binding; `ui/src` stays untouched until RFC 0003 is signed off,
so this surface lives at `ui/design-proof/`.

Unresolved: whether the catalog splits cached from upstream search results (RFC 0003
open question 2); pagination is drawn but not wired; no browser can run in this
container, so nothing here has been verified against a real render.
